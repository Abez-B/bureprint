BurePrint is an open-source, privacy-first mobile printing application for Android.

Key Features:
- Print documents, PDFs, photos, and web pages directly from your device.
- Local-first architecture: Zero telemetry, zero cloud tracking, zero remote servers.
- Automatic Wi-Fi network printer discovery via mDNS / DNS-SD / IPP (Internet Printing Protocol).
- Direct USB OTG printer support.
- Built-in multi-page preview, custom margins, multi-copy, and print settings.
- Merge multiple documents and export or print as a unified PDF.
- Saved documents stored directly in your device's organized BurePrint folder.

Privacy Policy:
BurePrint processes all documents locally on your device. No document contents, filenames, or printer credentials are ever transmitted to any third-party servers.
